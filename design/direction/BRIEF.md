# The line

Agreed with him on 14 October 2026, in his words, lightly tightened. Every sketch is judged against it.

> It takes time to get something. Attention to detail helps. The thing will be beautiful if you stop to notice.

What it asks of a design, read from his reactions so far:
- Something that comes together as you interact with it: "the parts all become a cohesive sum
  greater than its parts".
- Small details that reward noticing them.
- One idea, with everything on the page serving it. One place to look at a time.
- A human touch and some whimsy. Not a design agency's brand site, not a template.

Added after round 4: generous at a glance, deep on request. A visitor gets the whole picture in one
look; going deeper is optional and never the price of getting through. "even senior well respected
people in fields dont askf or that much."

Added after round 4: the page is complete and beautiful at rest. Interacting rewards you as you go,
but interaction is never what you must do to get the reward. "it should reward you as you interact.
the interact shouldnt be the thing that is required FOR the rward"
He tied this to v0: "that was one of the ways the bookshelf failed. it required you to react for the
reward (the info) vs the other way around."
