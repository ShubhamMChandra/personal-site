# The combined sketch

Built 14 October 2026 to hold every principle in design/direction/BRIEF.md at once.

The one idea: a small handful of coloured glass near his name, lit from behind, its coloured light
falling softly across the whole page. Where pieces overlap they make colours none of them has alone.

How it answers each principle:
- The line (time, detail, noticing): the pieces gather on arrival over a couple of seconds; the glass
  has small details (bevels, bubbles) that reward a closer look.
- One idea, one place to look: one cluster, one light; everything else is his words in that light.
- About how it feels to be in it: warm coloured light on paper, not glass as a subject.
- Generous at a glance: name, links, opening line and roles in the first look; no clicks needed.
- Complete at rest; interaction rewards: moving a finger moves the light; tapping a piece lifts it.
  Nothing is hidden behind an action.
- Comes together: the gathering on arrival, and the overlaps, where colours exist only together.
- Human touch: hand-cut edges, uneven colour, slow settle with no bounce.
