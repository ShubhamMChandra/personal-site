# Sketch round 2: brief

Written 14 October 2026, after his round 1 reactions (design/sketches/round-1/REACTIONS.md).

## What he liked, in his words
- Round 1 sketch 3 (hand-cut coloured film): "i really like the color and tactile feel of it. and the
  like glass on glass kind of feel. thats nice. a bit modern art for my taste just a bit."
- Round 1 sketch 5 (painted strokes): "made me smile a bit when i saw things moving as i went through
  it. the flow of it is nice, the colors are good albeit a tad muted". It reminded him of the film
  It's Kind of a Funny Story: its hand-drawn animation of the city, its surreal daydream sequences,
  "and the feeling of this like childlike wonder those images evoque."
- Loose signal, not to anchor on: Frieren, Jujutsu Kaisen, Chainsaw Man, Mr. Nobody, Scott Pilgrim,
  Wes Anderson.

## What lost
Text on a screen with a background effect ("web 1.0"); a sunset gradient ("tacky", "word art-y");
a striking image that doesn't respond ("just is there as digital art").

## Every round 2 sketch
- A light or mid-tone ground.
- A material you can almost touch. The hand is in the material itself, not in drawn notes.
- Something responds to him as he moves through the whole page, not only the first screen.
- Colour a step richer than round 1's sketch 5, and a step less gallery than its sketch 3.
- Childlike wonder, with the feel of hand-drawn daydream sequences.
- No captions naming the gesture.
- Same typefaces, grain and placeholder words as round 1, so the comparison is about material.

## The four
1. Glass (Claude): pieces of coloured glass that slide over each other as you scroll.
2. Paint (visionary): round 1's sketch 5, pushed further.
3. Layered paper (Claude): cut paper with real depth that moves like stop-motion.
4. Hand-drawn ink line (visionary): lines that wobble like hand-drawn animation, as you go through.

## Two more that reinvent the format

He added, while round 2 was being built: "but dont conform yourself to this. reinvent the wheel for
some!!" A separate visionary agent builds 5 and 6 with licence to break the format: no scrolling
page required, its own type, its own structure. What still holds: his words, wonder, warmth, made
by a person, responsive, no item theme, nothing childish, and the facts reachable in one tap.

## A seventh, from a mentor's note

A mentor picked out "I do better with things that take a little time to make sense of" and
suggested taking the visitor on a journey of depth, visually and in words. Sketch 7 (Claude): the
page opens as that one sentence; any phrase unfolds into more when tapped, as deep as you want to
go; each layer you open lays another tint of coloured glass over the words. One tap opens
everything, for anyone in a hurry. Every fact comes from the v0 site's copy; nothing is invented.
