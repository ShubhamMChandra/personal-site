# Sketch round 1: brief

Written 14 October 2026 while he naps. Source: design/direction/in-his-words.md and triads.md.

## What these are

High-fidelity mockups, at his request: "they can be high fidelity mockups since we're gonna do a lot
of reacting and refining and throwing stuff away." Each one tests a different *kind* of site, so his
reaction tells us which kind is more like him. He judges each with two questions: "which is more
like you?" and then "which part?". All of them are meant to be thrown away.

## What every sketch must hold (his words)

- Wonder and "bright childlike awe". Not "light, joyous and refined." Not childish either.
- Warm. Warmth wins when it is paired with depth, structure or craft.
- Rugged yet ritzy: an everyday shape in a fine material, worn in, nothing for show.
- Made by a person, with love and care. True to what it is.
- One light conceit: a single thing you can touch or drag, like the wave on andremarques.xyz or the
  notes on wam.team. It never stands between the visitor and the content.
- Abstract. No item theme (no watch, no bookshelf), no literal scene.

## What none of them may be

Stodgy, manicured, old world, staged, polished, refined, an event, tech-bro black, childish.

## Held constant, so the comparison is about kind

- The same two typefaces in every sketch.
- The same few words: his name, his city, and links. Sketches that need body text use his own v0 copy.
- The same grain over everything, for a worn-in surface.
- Each works by touch on a phone and by mouse on a desktop, and calms down under reduced motion.

## The four sketches

1. **Deep field.** A warm dark space much larger than the screen, full of soft glowing colour at
   different depths. Drag to look around it. Tests: awe through depth and light; night.
2. **Coloured glass.** The page is panes of saturated coloured glass, slightly irregular, as if cut
   by hand. Your finger is the light behind them. Tests: awe through colour and pattern; day.
3. **Dusk.** Scrolling moves the light from late afternoon into night, and small warm lights come on
   as it gets dark. The content sits along the way. Tests: the site as a journey through time.
4. **Plain page.** A text page in his own words, warm and readable, with one small warm light that
   follows your finger. Tests: content first, with almost no gesture.

## Open variables this round does not test

Type family, ground colour in isolation, photography, motion speed. Those come after a kind wins.

## Changed after an independent review (design-quality agent, given only his words)

- All sketches: removed every caption that named the gesture ("move the light", "keep going",
  "swipe sideways"). The index tells him each page has one thing to touch.
- 1: removed the pencil marks inside the prose (read as performed handmade); stronger lamp; the
  light now warms the words it falls on.
- 2: one gesture only (drag); removed the pencil circle, its label and the tap sparks; warm colour
  only; the first screen is shorter so the page shows sooner; lighter veil over the text.
- 3: kept against the reviewer's "cut", because it is the only bright, joyous one and he asked for
  bright. Six primaries became four deeper colours; idle motion halved.
- 4: about half as long (no full-screen sections); city lights come on earlier; a left light is
  fully bright at any time of day.
