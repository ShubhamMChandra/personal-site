I do better with things that take a little time to make sense of.

I’m drawn to interesting textures, small restaurants that don’t advertise themselves, and travel that doesn’t come with a gift shop. I like being in places that aren’t trying to perform for you. Street level feels honest: you’re walking, noticing, adjusting. In Kerala, that’s a fabric market, not a landmark.

Back home, I pay attention to craft. Clothes are an easy example: weave, weight, hand feel, drape. I’m not interested in labels as a proxy for taste. I’m interested in the work.
