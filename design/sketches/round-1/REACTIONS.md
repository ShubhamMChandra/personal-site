## Read, offered back to him

What 3 and 5 share: a light, mid-tone ground rather than a dark one; a physical medium you can
almost touch (cut film, paint strokes); the hand is in the medium itself, not in drawn notes; and
something moves in response to him as he goes through the page.
What lost: dark text pages with an effect behind them (1, 2); a sky gradient (4); a striking image
that doesn't respond (6).
Note: he loved gwern and Sivers in the archive, but text-first lost here. Content-first is not the
kind for his own site.
