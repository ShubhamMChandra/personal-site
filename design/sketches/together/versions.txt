v1	Paint and glass, dusk	Dusk: the same page after dark. Warm lamplight round the words, sodium-amber glass, a cool note.
v2	Paint and glass, leaves	Leaves: the glass gathers into a ring beside each passage, yellow at the top deepening to red, after the Goldsworthy still you liked.
v3	Paint and glass, ikat	Ikat: cotton-pale paint, and glass in indigo, madder and turmeric that crosses into a woven band, from the Kerala market and the dye colours you liked.
v4	Paint and glass, highlight	Highlight: the glass settles under the words you have reached, like the colour arriving under the words in round 2, sketch 2.
v5	Paint and glass, concrete	Concrete: cool board-marked greys laid flat like the Barbican, and one warm column of glass beside each passage, a lit window in a cold building.
