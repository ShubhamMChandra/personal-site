# Glass, rebuilt to hold the principles

His two favourite glass sketches, rebuilt so the glass is the page (design/direction/RECAP.md).

1. Settle when you stop (from round 2's sliding shards, "even more beautiful" but not cohesive).
   While you scroll, the shards slide apart over each other at their own depths. When you stop, they
   settle back into one composition around the passage you're reading. Complete at rest; the reward
   comes from stopping, which is the line taken literally.
2. Panes hold the page (from round 1's coloured film, "the color and tactile feel", "glass on glass",
   "a bit modern art"). Each part of the page sits on its own pane of coloured glass, overlapping its
   neighbours; the light behind them moves with your finger. Hand-cut panes, not geometric shapes.

Both: a generous page with everything at rest, the dye colours he liked, slow motion that feels made.
