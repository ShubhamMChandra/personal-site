I do better with things that take a little time to make sense of.

I’m drawn to interesting textures, small restaurants that don’t advertise themselves, and travel that doesn’t come with a gift shop. I like being in places that aren’t trying to perform for you. In Kerala, that’s a fabric market, not a landmark.

Back home, I pay attention to craft. Clothes are an easy example: weave, weight, hand feel, drape. Cooking is the same impulse. Start with something solid, then tinker until it tastes like mine.

I read a lot of sci-fi, especially the kind that starts fun and ends up making you stare at the ceiling for ten minutes.
