## The one lesson
One idea, and everything on the page serves it. One place to look at a time. Round 2 failed on
this four ways: scattered ("not cohesive"), "disjointed from the theme", "too much going on and its
not all central to a single conceit", "idk where to focus".

## Still true from rounds 1 and 2
- Wonder, "bright childlike awe", warmth, made by a person, "otherworldly within the real world".
- Something responds as he moves through it and makes him smile.
- Depth and "things that take a little time to make sense of" (his mentor), but cheap to explore:
  obvious, few steps, big payoff per step.
- Glass is the strongest material so far, but not required.
- Out: text with a background effect; sunset gradients; static art; a city map; comic or riso
  panels; kid-craft paper; candy-shop pastels (pink, butter yellow, baby blue); anything with
  nowhere to focus.
- The facts (roles, email) stay reachable in one tap or one glance.
