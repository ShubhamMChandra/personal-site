# Sketch round 4: brief

Judged against the line in design/direction/BRIEF.md: "It takes time to get something. Attention to
detail helps. The thing will be beautiful if you stop to notice."

Built one at a time and shown as each is ready, at his request.

1. The zoom, made by hand (Claude). Round 3's zoom, whose idea he liked "a lot", with the human
   touch and whimsy it lacked: each full stop is a tiny hand-drawn thing worth noticing (a button, a
   marigold, a small planet, a sun, a wax seal); zooming in shows its detail, then the next part of
   his life appears inside it. Painted washes instead of flat colour.
2. Connect the dots (Claude). Warm lights in a night sky, each a detail of his life; noticing them
   one by one draws a hand-drawn line between them until the whole drawing comes together and lights.

Context from round 3 and his photo: attention as focus; a small gift to someone who didn't expect
it; warm light against the dark at night, calm rather than loud.
